# Changelog

## 0.1.0-alpha.1 (unreleased)

First release. It's an alpha: any alpha may change the API, and each entry here says what changed and how to update. Depend on the exact version, `=0.1.0-alpha.1`; a plain `0.1.0-alpha.1` lets cargo take later alphas too. It pins each alpha it re-exports exactly, so a release of any of them comes as a release of pdfs, and takes pdf-tokenizer on its `0.2` line.

### Added

- The family re-exported whole: pdf-tokenizer `0.2`, pdf-objects, pdf-filters and pdf-crypt `=0.1.0-alpha.3`, pdf-color `=0.1.0-alpha.2`, and pdf-structure, pdf-content-streams, pdf-semantics, pdf-fonts, pdf-gfx, pdf-annots, pdf-forms and pdf-linearize `=0.1.0-alpha.1`. Each is at the root under two names: without the prefix (`pdfs::tokenizer`, `objects`, `filters`, `crypt`, `structure`, `color`, `content_streams`, `semantics`, `fonts`, `gfx`, `annots`, `forms` and `linearize`) and as the crate is named (`pdfs::pdf_gfx` and the rest, which remain). Both name the same crate.
- `open`, `from_bytes` and `Options`: a file opened by its path or from any source, as a viewer reads it by default (PDFium's recovery and rules, zlib-rs, no fault looked for). `Pdf` reads each object when it's asked for. `Pdf::unlock` opens it by a password after.
- `Pdf::pages`, `Pages::dests`, `Pdf::open_action`, `Page`, `Page::geom`, `Page::annots`, `Page::plan_flatten`, `Page::content`, `Page::interpret` and `Page::encode`: a file's pages and destinations, each page with what it inherits, its 2D geometry (`PageGeom`), its annotations (`Annot`), its streams joined (`Content`) with their operations, its graphics and text state interpreted (`Summary`), and its content and Form XObjects re-encoded (`EncodedPage`).
- `Page::text`, `Page::text_page`, `Page::search` and `Page::fonts`: the strings a page's content shows, in its fonts, its forms followed (`Shown`), spatial reading-order text, selection quads and search (`pdf_gfx::TextPage` and `TextMatch` under `text-layout`), and the fonts its resources name (`NamedFont`), each read once a file.
- `Pdf::version`, `encryption`, `info`, `claims`, `metadata`, `outlines`, `page_labels`, `embedded_files`, `associated_files`, `optional_content`, `catalog_view`, `linearized` and `stream`: what the file is and says of itself, its bookmarks, page labels, attachments, optional content layers, and viewer preferences, and any stream's data decoded. `Pdf::reading` gives the reading, for the rest.
- `Document`: a new document (`Document::new`) or a file edited (`Pdf::edit`), its pages added with a `ContentWriter` each (`add_page`), page IDs inspected (`page_ids`), annotations added or flattened (`add_annot`, `flatten_page`), document structures set (`set_info`, `set_metadata`, `set_outlines`, `set_page_labels`, `add_embedded_file`, `set_catalog_view`), what they draw with written once a file, the standard 14 fonts by name (`standard_font`) and claims as XMP (`claim`).
- `Document::embed_font`, `Document::embed_font_with` and `EmbeddedFont`, with the `embed` feature: a TrueType program embedded in a document to draw Unicode text in. `EmbeddedFont::res` is the resource a page's content names it by, and `EmbeddedFont::codes` a string's codes in it, each character's glyph recorded as it's drawn. With `text-layout` it's a `pdf_gfx::LayoutFont`, pdf-gfx's `ProgramFont` over its program and the glyphs its document records, so `Canvas::text_box` lays text out in it, and `Canvas::text_box_in` and pdf-forms' `appearance::text` in a `pdf_gfx::WithFallback` of another font and it. `EmbeddedFont::record` takes glyphs drawn in its program by what didn't record them there, as pdf-forms' `appearance::text_with_fallback` returns them (`MadeWithFallback::glyphs`) given `EmbeddedFont::res` and `EmbeddedFont::program` as its fallback, so the next save writes the font with them; a glyph past the program's is refused, none recorded. Each save writes the font at the number the document reserved for it, its program cut to the glyphs drawn by then (pdf-fonts' `embed::write`) and each of its streams deflated, so a save after more is drawn rewrites the same objects with more glyphs, and what was drawn before still shows. `Error::Embed` wraps pdf-fonts' `embed::Error`, and `prelude` gains `EmbeddedFont`.
- `Document::save`, `save_with`, `save_linearized` and `save_update`: a document saved whole, linearized, or as an update appended to the file read, each with a `_to` form that writes into memory. `encrypt` and `decrypt` say how it's encrypted.
- `page`, `file` and `build`: each recipe as a function over the family's traits, for a reading or a document of the caller's own.
- `Limits`, every crate's caps in one value, with `font_budget`, and `Error`, every crate's error wrapped as it is.
- `prelude`: the facade's types and calls, and the family's items they take and give: `Canvas`, `Color`, `ContentWriter`, `EncodedPage`, `Matrix`, `PageGeom`, `Paint`, `Point`, `Quad`, `Rect`, `Res`, `Rotation`, `SearchOpts`, `TextAlign`, `TextBoxStyle`, `TextPage`, `TextPageOpts`, `VerticalAlign`, `WrapMode`, `Std14`, `Font`, `Shown`, `ShownString`, `Unread`, `PdfBox`, `Claims`, `Info`, `Verdict`, `Encryption`, `Encoded`, `Password`, `Permissions`, `Protection`, `Rules`, `Version`, `Dict`, `Object`, `ObjectId` and `Why`, and what pdf-annots' builders take: `BuiltAnnot`, `RedactMark`, `DefaultAppearance`, `Quadding`, `QuadPoints` and `PaintStyle`.
- Features, each the crates' own: `programs`, `text-layout`, `clock` and `embed` by default, and `image-ends`, `predefined-cmaps`, `simd` and `tracing`. `embed` turns on pdf-fonts', pdf-gfx's and pdf-forms', and no `text-layout`.

### Security

- `Document::encrypt` is AES-256, revision 6, its key and salts from the operating system. It's written under the document's own version, 1.7 for a new one, and `encrypt`'s docs say what a checker by version finds. An edited encrypted file is saved under its own encryption unless `decrypt` or `encrypt` says otherwise. A locked file isn't edited.
- Under an edited RC4 file's own encryption, new plaintext at a keystream the file used is refused unless the caller asks (`Document::reuse_rc4_keystreams`, for an update and a linearized save alike), and what's so written is listed.
- A save never writes over the file a `Pdf` reads by position (`Error::SameFile`), and an update is appended only to the file read, as it was (`Error::NotTheBase`). Neither is a durable write.
- What's behind a password reads as locked, never as ciphertext. No `Debug` holds a password, a key or a file's content, and a span's fields are numbers.
- What a file permits once a password opens it binds the caller, not the facade: opened by its user's password, a file that withholds changes is edited and saved, and `decrypt` saves it in the clear. `Pdf::encryption` gives the permissions and which password opened it.
- The `insecure-test-writer` feature, for tests and fuzzing, turns on pdf-structure's and pdf-linearize's test writer, which draws a new encryption's key from a seeded generator. A build that ships leaves it off.

### Notes

- It needs Rust 1.92. Nothing panics on any file, and past a cap a call says so.
- A page's or a form's stream that doesn't decode, damaged or through a filter the reader doesn't take, is read as none and said (`Concat::undecoded`, `Unread::Undecoded`), where PDFium reads its data as written.
- A page's box is written as the content writer writes a number, one that isn't finite as 0.
- `Options`, `Limits`, `Error` and `NamedFont` are `#[non_exhaustive]`, so each can grow in a minor release. The crates' own types keep their own policies.
- Only a program with TrueType outlines is embedded: one whose outlines are CFF's, CFF2's or bitmaps alone is refused (`Error::Embed`), as is one whose licence restricts it unless `embed_font_with`'s options say the caller has the right. Text is drawn in an embedded font a character at a time, without shaping: no ligatures, no kerning, no reordering of right-to-left text. Justified text in one is laid out flush left, as word spacing applies to no two-byte code.
- An `EmbeddedFont` is its document's: drawn on another's pages, it names an object there that isn't the font. A font embedded and never drawn in is written all the same, its `.notdef` alone. A program `embed_font` takes is one every save can cut, as pdf-fonts' `Program::open` refuses any other. `page_ids`, `add_annot`, `flatten_page` and `set_outlines` place the pages added but not the fonts, which a save writes as the glyphs drawn by then say.
- `Page::plan_flatten` and `Document::flatten_page` keep `/Redact` marks on the page, neither drawn nor removed, unless `FlattenOpts::with_redactions(true)` asks for them: flattening never removes the content under a mark, so a mark painted onto the page would only look like a redaction.
- `Document::add_annot` binds a built redaction's `/RO` overlay as `/AP /R` beside `/N`, as pdf-annots' `BuiltAnnot::write_into` does, so a viewer shows what applying the mark leaves while the pointer is over it.
- An annotation built in a font of its own (`BuiltAnnot::free_text_with_font`, `redact_with_font`, which keep it as `BuiltAnnot::da_font`) has the font put in the form's default resources by `Document::add_annot` and `build::add_annot`, under the name its `/DA` gives, or the first free one after it (pdf-forms' `default_resources::font_name`) where that's another font's, which `/DA` is then written to name, the name of its last `Tf` alone replaced and every other byte of it kept; so a viewer that makes an appearance of its own from `/DA`, or applies a redaction from `/OverlayText` and `/DA`, draws in the same font. The form, its default resources and their fonts are extended where they're held, directly or by reference, and a document with no form gains one with an empty `/Fields` and `/DR` alone (ISO 32000-2, Table 224, requires `/Fields`). A font given as a resource of objects is written as `build::resource` writes one, once a document by `Document::add_annot`, once an annotation by `build::add_annot`. A `/DA` naming no font registers nothing; a document with no catalog is refused (`Error::NoCatalog`) where there's a font to register.
- `Page::plan_flatten` and `Document::flatten_page` keep a kept redaction mark's `/Popup` with it.
- An annotation's text is drawn in an embedded font through pdf-annots, with `embed` and `text-layout`: `pdfs::annots::BuiltAnnot::free_text_with_font` and `redact_with_font` take `EmbeddedFont::res` and the font itself (a `LayoutFont`), and `Document::add_annot` writes the `/AP /N` and `/RO` streams they built, each naming the font's number in its `/Resources`, which the save fills with the glyphs drawn. A character the program has no glyph for is an error from the builder, with nothing added. The font is put in the form's default resources under the name the annotation's `/DA` gives, as the entry above says.
- What isn't here: raster images, forms, signatures, fonts embedded from CFF programs, shaping, claims merged into a file's XMP, and a durable save.
