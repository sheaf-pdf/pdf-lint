# Changelog

## 0.1.0-alpha.1 (unreleased)

First release. It's an alpha: any alpha may change the API, and each entry here says what changed and how to update. Depend on the exact version, `=0.1.0-alpha.1`; a plain `0.1.0-alpha.1` lets cargo take later alphas too. Its unit tests don't build from the package. They take their files from pdf-fixtures, which is never published. They read them through pdf-structure, not yet on crates.io.

### Added

- `Font::read`: a font dictionary read as PDFium (chromium/8152) reads it, over pdf-objects' `Resolve` and `Streams`. Type 1, MMType1, TrueType, Type 3 and Type 0 fonts, with their embedded CMaps and their `/ToUnicode`.
- `Font`'s readings: a string's codes (`Font::codes`, a `Codes` iterator), each code's CID, width, vertical metrics and text, how far the font's glyphs reach, and a glyph's box. `Font::approx` says which of them are guessed.
- `Fonts` and `FontScope`: each font of a source read once, kept for every page that draws in it and charged to each page's budget, and the fonts one interpretation of content picks, by name.
- `shown`: the strings a page's content shows, in order, each in its font, as PDFium reads them for its text, with no layout. It reads `Tf`, `gs` where its ExtGState's `/Font` picks a font, `q` and `Q`, `Tj`, `TJ`, `'` and `"`, and follows `Do` of a form in its own state, to `Limits::forms` deep and `Limits::form_uses` times a call. `Shown` holds each string (`ShownString`) and what wasn't read (`Unread`): a form too deep or drawn too often, content past a cap, a form or a font past the budget or behind a password, a graphics state behind one, and a stream whose data doesn't decode, which PDFium reads as written.
- The `programs` feature, on by default: a font's program read through skrifa, for `Font::char_box`, the font's heights and the text its encoding leaves to it. `Font::read_for_text` and `Fonts::for_text` read none.
- `Encoding`, `glyph_text`, `name_code_point` and `cp1252`: a simple font's encodings (D.2, D.5, D.6), and the text a glyph's name stands for.
- `Std14`: the standard 14 fonts, found by a `/BaseFont` as PDFium takes it, with Adobe's metrics. `Std14::NOTICE` and `Std14::afm_text` are for a binary's list of notices.
- `Std14::dict`, `Std14::codes`, `Encoding::code` and `Unencodable`: a standard 14 font's dictionary, and text in its codes, which read back as the text given. A character with no code is refused, never replaced.
- `Fault` and `Error`: where a font departs from the standard, told with its place to pdf-objects' `fault::Sink`, and what stops a reading. A cap of the reading's own and what the build hasn't are told apart from the file's faults.
- `Limits`: caps on a CMap's chain, a Type 1 program's scan, a program's glyphs and how deep and how often `shown` follows forms, beside pdf-content-streams' caps for what a font and `shown` lex as content.
- The `embed` feature, off by default, and its module `embed`: a TrueType program embedded in a PDF (9.9). `Program::open` opens a TrueType or OpenType font, or a face of a collection of them, whose outlines are TrueType's. `Program::codes` gives a string's codes for a Type 0 font over `/Identity-H`: two bytes a character, the number of the glyph the program's `cmap` maps it to. `Program::glyph`, `width`, `metrics`, `glyph_count` and `embedding` read the rest a layout and a caller need.
- `embed::Glyphs`: the glyphs a document draws in a program, each with the text it stands for. `Glyphs::add` records a string's as its codes are made, and `Glyphs::insert` a glyph a caller draws by its number. `Glyphs::merge` joins glyphs gathered apart (each appearance's, each thread's), a glyph both keep keeping the smaller text, so the join is the same in any order and one font written of it serves them all. `Glyphs::iter` gives each glyph's number in the program and its text, in number order, for a caller that cuts the program itself and writes its own `/ToUnicode`.
- `embed::write` and `embed::Options`: the font as a PDF writes it, its objects in the order `Res::of` takes a resource's. A Type 0 font over `/Identity-H`; its CIDFontType2 with `/W` and `/CIDToGIDMap`; a `/FontDescriptor` from the program; the program cut to the glyphs drawn, `.notdef` and the glyphs their composites draw, as `/FontFile2`; a `/ToUnicode` CMap; and a `/CIDSet` where `Options::cid_set` asks for one. `Options::whole` embeds every glyph, under `/CIDToGIDMap /Identity`.
- `embed::Error`, `embed::Outlines` and `embed::Unmapped`: why a program isn't opened or written, and a character it has no glyph for. `embed::Embedding` and `embed::Level`: what a program's licence allows, from its `OS/2` table's `fsType`, and `Embedding::allows`, whether `write` takes it under given `Options`.
- The `predefined-cmaps` feature, off by default: Adobe's predefined CMaps, from hayro-cmap's bundle. The `tracing` feature, off by default: a span over a font read, and within it over its program's opening, its CMap's and its `/ToUnicode`'s parsing, and a Type 3 font's CharProcs; a span over a walk of what content shows; and, with `embed`, a span over a font written.

### Notes

- It's built on pdf-objects `=0.1.0-alpha.3` and pdf-content-streams `=0.1.0-alpha.1`, both re-exported whole and none of their items by name. `Limits::content` is pdf-content-streams' type. It needs Rust 1.92.
- Where it reads a case otherwise than PDFium, the repository's `tests/pdfium/differ.tsv` lists it and why.
- `Fault::UseCMap`, `Fault::ToUnicode` and `Fault::ProgramMalformed` are told too of a stream whose filters the reader doesn't decode, which is the reader's and not the file's. A later alpha may tell that apart.
- A reading past the caller's budget fails with `Error::TooLarge`. A stream past a cap of what gives it fails it with `Error::Unreadable`, the caller's to know, as one behind a password does. Nothing is told to the sink after that. Either way the budget is as it was.
- Nothing in this crate panics on what a file holds, nor on a program `embed` is given. A font's program is parsed by skrifa 0.50 and read-fonts 0.47.
- It's licensed `(MIT OR Apache-2.0) AND FTL`, as `src/program.rs` translates some of FreeType's code, under `FTL.TXT`. The fonts' data keeps its own terms, which the README lists. So does what `embed` brings in: subsetter's notice credits code it adapts from ttf-parser and fonttools, under MIT.
- With `embed`, a program is cut by subsetter 0.2.6, without its `variable-fonts` feature, so a variable font is embedded at its default instance. It's pinned to that version, as `Program::open` checks a program as it reads one: another comes with a release of this crate. Only TrueType outlines are embedded: a program whose outlines are CFF's, CFF2's or bitmaps alone is refused (`embed::Error::Outlines`), as are most `.otf` faces and the CJK collections built on CFF. A later alpha may take CFF.
- `Program::open` refuses, beside a table it needs that doesn't read, what a subset would make too much of or fail on: a composite glyph naming a glyph past the program's count, `loca` entries whose glyphs overlap, `name` records past what a `name` table holds, units per em outside OpenType's 16 to 16,384, and a table directory out of order (`embed::Error::Directory`). So what's done with an opened program is linear in its size, and there's no cap to set. It refuses too what a subset would refuse for some glyph, or write wrong: a glyph `loca` doesn't place or whose data isn't a whole glyph's, an `hmtx` without a glyph's side bearing, an `hhea` past its 36 bytes, a `maxp` of a version but 1.0, and a `post` of version 2.0 that doesn't name every glyph. So any glyphs of a program opened are cut, and `write` refuses only a `Glyphs` past its glyphs and what its licence restricts.
- A program's licence is kept: one restricted to its owner's leave, or to its bitmaps, is refused unless `Options::licensed` says the caller has the right, which `Embedding::allows` tells before anything is drawn, and one that asks to be embedded whole is, whatever `Options` says. `Level` orders levels least restrictive first. A program embedded whole is every glyph, but only the tables a cut has: no `cmap`, `OS/2`, or layout or variations' tables.
- A code is the glyph's number in the program, never in the subset, so codes drawn before more text is drawn still show their glyphs when the font is written again. A subset's tag is six capitals from a digest of the program and the glyphs it's cut to, so the same glyphs are written in the same bytes.
- Text is drawn a character at a time, without shaping: no ligatures, no kerning, no reordering of right-to-left text. A character the program has no glyph for is refused (`Unmapped`), never drawn as `.notdef`. Text is kept a glyph, not a character: two characters a program draws with one glyph show one code, which reads back as the smaller of their texts.
- `embed::write`'s streams are unfiltered: filtering is for what writes the file.
- `Std14`, `GlyphBox` and `VerticalMetrics` are exhaustive on purpose. Every other enum and every struct with public fields is `#[non_exhaustive]`, `embed`'s among them, so each can grow in a minor release. `Fault`'s variants aren't, so a fault can be built and compared.
