# Changelog

## 0.1.0-alpha.1 (unreleased)

The first release, and an alpha. Its unit tests don't build from the package. They take their files from pdf-fixtures, which is never published, and read them through pdf-structure, not yet on crates.io.

- **Licensed `(MIT OR Apache-2.0) AND FTL`,** as `src/program.rs` translates some of FreeType's code: `FTL.TXT`, and the README's list of what's taken.
- **Ids are pdf-objects' struct.** `Font::id`, each `Fault`'s id, `Place::object` and what `Streams::decoded` is asked are pdf-objects' `ObjectId`, a struct of `number` and `generation`, where it was the pair `(u32, u16)`: `(1, 0)` is `ObjectId::new(1, 0)`, `id.0` is `id.number`.
- **`Place` and `Sink` are pdf-objects'.** A `Fault` is told to pdf-objects' `fault::Sink<Fault>` with its `fault::Place`, the types pdf-semantics' and pdf-color's faults are told to, so one sink can take all three; `Place` and `Sink` are no longer this crate's, nor at its root. pdf-objects is re-exported whole (`pdf_fonts::pdf_objects`), so a sink of one's own names them at the version this crate takes. Code follows: `use pdf_fonts::{Place, Sink}` is `use pdf_objects::fault::{Place, Sink}`, or `pdf_fonts::pdf_objects::fault`, `impl Sink for S` is `impl Sink<pdf_fonts::Fault> for S`, a bound `S: Sink` is `S: Sink<Fault>` and `&mut dyn Sink` is `&mut dyn Sink<Fault>`; a `Vec<(Place, Fault)>` keeps them as before. What a fault's place names, the descriptor, the CIDFont or a CMap's stream where it's another than the font dictionary, is on `Fault`'s module now.
- **`Streams` is pdf-objects', and the `structure` feature is gone.** What gives a font its streams' data is pdf-objects' `Streams`, which a pdf-structure `Reading` implements in pdf-structure, as its `Document` and `WithReading` do, so a font is read over a `&Reading` with no feature of this crate's: `structure`, and with it the optional dependency on pdf-structure, are gone, and the `pdfium` tests run without it. Code follows: `pdf_fonts::{Streams, With}` are `pdf_objects::{Streams, WithStreams}`, reached as `pdf_fonts::pdf_objects` too (`WithStreams` is named for what it joins, objects with their streams' data, and the docs' and README's examples make a font's objects with it), and `Decoded` is gone, `decoded` giving a `Result<Cow<[u8]>, Unreadable>`: `Data(data)` is `Ok(Cow::Owned(data))`, or a `Cow::Borrowed` where the data is held; `TooLarge` is an `Err` of `Why::Limit`; `None` one of `Why::Damaged`, or `Why::Filter` for filters the reader doesn't decode; `Unreadable(u)` is `Err(u)`. A `WithStreams`'s function gives `Result<Vec<u8>, Unreadable>`. A font reads them as it read `Decoded`: data within what it asked for is charged; `Why::Limit` is `Error::TooLarge`, past its budget or a cap of what gives the stream, where an `Unreadable` of `Why::Limit` for a stream, more filters than a reading takes, was `Error::Unreadable`, the one thing the two kinds of answer can't keep apart; `Why::Damaged` and `Why::Filter` are no stream, and what the font makes of none is what's told, as for `Decoded::None`; anything else is `Error::Unreadable`. pdf-objects tells the two apart now, `Why::Limit` past the limit asked for and `Why::Capped` past a cap of what gives the stream, or of what resolves an object: a font reads both as `Error::TooLarge`, as it read the one, and a later version may make a cap met `Error::Unreadable`, the caller's to know.
- **A file's font, as read.** `Font::read`, over pdf-objects' `Resolve` and `Streams`, and what it gives: `id`, `loaded`, `kind` (`FontKind`, `Descendant`), `is_vertical`, `approx` (`FontApprox`), `program` (`FontProgram`), `cost`, `codes`, `word_spaced`, `cid`, `width`, `width_guessed`, `width_guessed_in`, `byte_widths`, `vertical` (`VerticalMetrics`), `metrics`, `glyph_box`, `char_box`, `charge_outlines`, `outlined`, `code_text`, `text`, `extent`. Type 1, MMType1, TrueType, Type 3 and Type 0 fonts, each as PDFium reads it; a standard 14 font by Adobe's metrics; embedded CMaps over those `/UseCMap` names, which PDFium doesn't read, so anything taken from them is said in `FontApprox::codes`; `/ToUnicode`; predefined CMaps with `predefined-cmaps`.
- **As PDFium reads them, hostile values too.** Each value of a font's dictionary by PDFium's accessor for it, as of chromium/8125, held to PDFium's answers case by case: `true` as 1, what's no number 0, a real cut from its 32-bit float, past 32 bits 0; a simple font's widths in 16 bits, a CID font's `/DW` and vertical metrics too, its `/W` in 32; a reference followed once, and a dictionary's one more, a stream's dictionary read as one; `/Differences` by one reader, what's no number or name setting the code; `/Flags` for a font of capitals only and for its base encoding, `Ascent` and `Descent`, `FontBBox` and held within it only where its top is above its bottom; a fixed-pitch standard font's widths starting at its pitch; Type 3's `/FontMatrix` of six or none, `/Widths`, `/FontBBox` and every `d0` or `d1` in a CharProc; a CMap PDFium doesn't know by two bytes each, each its CID, with no CID's text; a CMap's ranges that end past 16 bits mapping only codes past them, by where they end; and a predefined CMap's codes below the space with CID 0; a TrueType font PDFium reads as GBK by its name; a `/ToUnicode` CMap's blocks taken whole or not at all by their counts and codes, a later mapping over an earlier, its values in 16-bit units with U+FFFF and an empty one no text, what it misses in a Type 0 font its CID's text, and an Adobe-*-UCS2 it names read for what it maps none of; a TrueType font named Symbol with no program by PDFium's table, so its Delta, Omega and mu are Greek.
- **Programs,** with `programs`, on by default, through skrifa: `/FontFile`, `/FontFile2` and `/FontFile3` by the first that's a stream, told apart by their bytes; `char_box`, each glyph's outline box as PDFium's FreeType gives it, a code mapped to its glyph as `LoadGlyphMap` maps it; `outlined`, `charge_outlines`, `FontProgram`; heights from the face where a descriptor lacks them; the text PDFium's encoding leaves to a program, by its glyphs' names or its charmaps; a lower case with no glyph taking its capital's, in a font of capitals; a program FreeType wouldn't load read as none. `Font::read_for_text` and `Fonts::for_text` read none, whatever the build's features. `Fault::ProgramMalformed`. `/Differences`, `/BaseEncoding` and `/Encoding` have the one reading, for text and for glyphs. `Limits`' `font_fuel`, `glyph_fuel`, `composite_glyphs` and `composite_depth` bound them, and an accented character's components, each looked up among all the glyphs, are charged to the fuel. skrifa's bound on a composite's components and read-fonts' on a charstring's operations, 2,048 and a million, aren't FreeType's: past one, a box is none where PDFium draws it. A multiple master font's weights and its private dictionary's `/BuildCharArray` are read for its othersubrs, as FreeType reads them. A CID-keyed CFF's CID given twice is its lowest glyph, as FreeType takes it. An OpenType font's CFF as FreeType reads it: its glyphs' names by its charset, whatever its `post` says, a Unicode charmap of them where it has none, its encoding's charmap, and a CID as its glyph, not by its charset. An SFNT's format by its tag, not by the tables it has.
- **Where a program is read otherwise than FreeType reads it,** a box is none and `FontApprox::boxes` says so: a `loca` of a format neither 0 nor 1, long metrics that don't fit `hmtx` or none, a `cmap` whose records don't fit, a glyph with data that draws no point. A glyph past the program's is PDFium's box of no glyph, and a `loca` entry past its table is charged nothing.
- **What's read in WinAnsiEncoding's empty codes:** the bullet, as ISO 32000-2's D.2 and PDFium have it, in `Encoding::glyph` and `Encoding::text`, so `Std14`'s width is the bullet's.
- **What's read at MacRomanEncoding's 0xF0:** the Apple logo, `apple`, U+F8FF, as PDFium's table has it, where ISO 32000-2's D.2 lists nothing.
- **A stream's data,** asked of pdf-objects' `Streams`, a type and a reference to it alike, which a pdf-structure `Reading` is and pdf-objects' `WithStreams` makes of a resolver and a function (above). A font refuses data past what it asked for.
- **Errors and faults.** `Error`, `TooLarge` or `Unreadable`; `Fault`, told to a sink with its place (pdf-objects' `fault::Sink` and `fault::Place`, above), as what's passed over is told, the same read whatever the sink. A CMap's chain of more streams than `Limits::cmaps`, a cap of the reading's, is `Fault::TooManyCMaps`, told in the first stream past it, apart from the file's `Fault::UseCMap`, a loop or a stream of the chain that doesn't read, as one as long as the cap that names an object the file hasn't is; `Fault::ToUnicode`'s words say it's of a `/ToUnicode` that's no stream or doesn't decode, both of which it's told of. Neither changes what's read, nor `FontApprox`.
- **Each font read once.** `Fonts` (`for_text`, `get`, `stock`, `scope`), `FontScope` (`font`, `stock`, `get`), `FontId`.
- **`Limits` whole:** `content`, `cmaps`, `type1_work`, and for programs, `font_fuel`, `glyph_fuel`, `composite_glyphs`, `composite_depth`.
- **Encodings.** `Encoding` (`#[non_exhaustive]`): StandardEncoding, WinAnsiEncoding, MacRomanEncoding, and Symbol's and ZapfDingbats' own, each a table of its own, not read from the metrics, with `named`, `glyph` and `text`. `cp1252`, Windows-1252's character for a code.
- **Glyph names.** `glyph_text`, by the Adobe Glyph List's rules, and `name_code_point`, as FreeType's glyph name list reads a name, which PDFium copies. The two lists are in `assets`, unchanged.
- **The standard 14.** `Std14`, with `ALL`, `name`, `named`, by PDFium's names as of chromium/8125, `metrics`, `glyph_width`, `glyph_box`, `drawn_otherwise`, `afm_text` and `NOTICE`. The metrics are Adobe's, in `assets/afm`, with their kerning data and blank lines removed, each file saying so and carrying Adobe's notice, with the set's `MustRead.html`. `FontMetrics`, with `new`, and `Rect`, what they're given in.
- **Type 1 programs,** with `programs`, scanned first, as read-fonts reads them, a name that isn't UTF-8 by its bytes as Latin-1, under `Limits::type1_work`, which counts its tokens' bytes and the names of its `/Encoding` looked for among its glyphs.
- **Predefined CMaps.** `predefined-cmaps`, a feature on hayro-cmap.
- **pdf-objects and pdf-content-streams, re-exported whole** (`pdf_fonts::pdf_objects`, `pdf_fonts::pdf_content_streams`), none of their items by name: what a font is read over and told to names pdf-objects' items at the version this crate takes (`pdf_fonts::pdf_objects::Dict`), and `Limits::content` is pdf-content-streams' type, so each crate's version is part of this one's API.
- **`Codes`,** what `Font::codes` gives: an iterator a caller can name and hold, fused and cloned, its `size_hint` holding how many codes are left (each a byte to four of the string, and exactly where each takes as many, as a simple font's do), its `Debug` where it is and how much is left, never the string.
- **A `Debug` that prints no file's text.** `Font`'s says what it is (`id`, `kind`, `loaded`, whether it's vertical, `program`, `approx`, `cost`, `metrics`, `extent`) and how many widths, CMap ranges and `/ToUnicode` ranges it holds, never a mapping's text, an encoding's names or a glyph's box.
- **`Hash`** on `Fault`, `FontKind`, `Descendant`, `FontProgram`, `FontApprox` and `Encoding`, so a caller can count them by kind.
- **Exhaustive on purpose:** `Std14`, the standard's 14, with `ALL` in the order of their discriminants, and `Rect` and `VerticalMetrics`, values of fixed parts, each saying so; every other public enum, and every struct with public fields, is `#[non_exhaustive]`. `Rect` is normalized as this crate gives one and as `Rect::new` makes one.
- **`#[must_use]`** where a result dropped is a mistake, and **`const fn`** where the body allows: `Rect::new`, `FontMetrics::new`, `FontApprox::any`, `Encoding::named`, `cp1252`, `Std14::name` and `afm_text`, and `Font`'s `id`, `loaded`, `kind`, `is_vertical`, `approx`, `program`, `cost`, `codes`, `width_guessed`, `outlined`, `metrics` and `extent`. Each `Result` says in its docs what fails it.
- **A code found among its ranges by their last codes alone.** A Type 0 font's widths and vertical metrics by `/W` and `/W2`, an embedded CMap's CIDs and a `/ToUnicode`'s text are found by a search over each range's last code, kept apart from its first and what it maps to, where the search read each range whole. A test holds a code's value to the range that wins it, the first given or the last, over ranges made at random, overlapping, joining and ending at the last code there is. The same widths, CIDs and text.
- **A code's text read from the encoding, with no `/ToUnicode` read, without asking a CMap of nothing.** `Font::code_text` and `Font::text` of a font whose `/ToUnicode` wasn't read go to its encoding's text straight away, where each code was first looked up in the empty CMap that stands for the missing one: a simple font of the standard 14 or of no `/ToUnicode` reads its text with no call into the CMap at all. The same text.
