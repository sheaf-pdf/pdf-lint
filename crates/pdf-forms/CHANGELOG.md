# Changelog

## 0.1.0-alpha.1 (unreleased)

First release. Until `0.1.0` stabilizes, alpha releases may include breaking API changes documented here; depend on the exact version (`=0.1.0-alpha.1`).

### Added

- `Form`, `Form::read`, `Form::read_with`, and `Form::from_dict`: a catalog's `/AcroForm` with its field tree read under `Rules::Iso` or `Rules::Pdfium` (as of chromium/8152), with the form's `/DA`, `/Q`, `/NeedAppearances`, `/SigFlags`, `/XFA` presence, and default resources (`Form::resources`, `Form::font`, `Form::font_names`).
- `Field`, `FieldType`, `FieldKind`, `FieldFlags`, and `FieldValue`: each field with its effective (own or inherited) type, flags, value, default value, default appearance, quadding, and maximum length, and its kind by the rules read under (a button with both the push button and radio flags is PDFium's radio button, ISO 32000-2's push button); under `Rules::Pdfium` a field with no `/MaxLen` of its own or an ancestor's takes the first of its widgets' own, the first widget whose dictionary has the entry, as PDFium (as of chromium/8152) reads it, where ISO 32000-2 gives the entry to fields alone (a `/MaxLen` of `null` on the field or an ancestor is one, and the widgets aren't read, as PDFium finds it); `Form::fields`, `Form::roots`, `Form::terminals`, `Form::full_name`, and `Form::find` (a scan that builds no name). A `/V` or `/DV` that reaches nothing (null, or a reference to no object or to null) is an absent one, and the parent's is inherited, as every other inherited entry is read.
- `Widget`, `Widget::annot`, and `Widget::annot_with`: a field's widgets, separate or merged with the field, each read as a `pdf_annots::Annot`.
- `Field::options`, `Field::options_with`, and `FieldOption`: a field's `/Opt`, a choice field's options (each an export value and the label shown for it) or a button field's export values, an item of another type kept in its place as an option of no text, and told; `Field::top_index` (`/TI`); `Field::selected` and `Field::selected_with` (`/I`); and `Field::selection`, `Field::selection_with`, and `selection`, the options a value selects as PDFium (as of chromium/8152) finds them, over plain data too: `/I` where it agrees with `/V`, else the options `/V` names. `/Opt`, `/TI`, and `/I` are the field's own or inherited under either rules, found with the field and decoded only when they're asked for, within `Limits::items` (`Cap::Items`): `Form::read` decodes none of them, and an `/Opt` or `/I` that can't be read fails the call that asks for it, or is told by it, not `Form::read`.
- `Widget::states`, `Widget::states_with`, and `WidgetStates`: a widget's on state, `/AS`, and whether it has an `Off` appearance, read from `/AP` and `/AS` alone.
- The `group` module: a check box or radio button field's rules as pure functions of plain data (`Group`, `GroupWidget`, `GroupKind`), with no feature: `Group::press`, `Group::states_for`, `Group::states_for_export`, `Group::value`, `Group::export`, `Group::state_name`, `Group::on_state`, `Group::export_value`, and `on_state_name`, the name a made appearance's on state goes under; `Field::group`, `Field::group_with`, and `FieldGroup` read a field's group and its widgets' states. A check box field is always in unison, and in unison a widget goes with those of its on state and export value, as PDFium (as of chromium/8152) has it; a radio button pressed while on turns off where the field hasn't NoToggleToOff, and `/V` is the on widget's state's name (`Off` with none), as ISO 32000-2 has them, where PDFium keeps the radio button on and, with `/Opt`, writes the widget's index or leaves `/V` as it was. Held to PDFium's own presses, recorded as cases.
- `appearance::check_box`, `appearance::radio_button`, `appearance::ToggleButton`, `appearance::ToggleAppearances`, and `appearance::Mark` (`text-layout`): a check box's and a radio button's appearances made from plain data as PDFium (as of chromium/8152) makes them, the normal and the down appearance of the on state and of `Off`, each held to the appearances PDFium makes; but the mark is the ZapfDingbats glyph the caption names (`Mark::from_caption`), drawn by name, centred on the box less its border and sized to PDFium's own shape for it (or at the size `/DA` gives), where PDFium draws shapes of its own; and an `Off` that would paint nothing isn't made, so it stays absent where PDFium writes an empty stream. A radio button whose mark is the circle has PDFium's round frame. The down appearances' background is each component less 0.25 (`0.75 0.75 0.75 rg` with none), a beveled border's shades exchanged and an inset one's black and white.
- `appearance::push_button`, `appearance::PushButton`, and `appearance::PushAppearances` (`text-layout`): a push button's normal, rollover, and down appearances (the latter two where its highlighting is push or toggle), its caption on one line centred in the box less its border and clipped there, at `/DA`'s size or automatic, as PDFium makes them.
- `appearance::widget_toggle`, `appearance::WidgetToggle`, `appearance::toggle_for_widget`, `appearance::MadeToggle`, `appearance::widget_push`, `appearance::WidgetPush`, `appearance::push_for_widget`, and `appearance::MadePush` (`text-layout`): a button widget read from what `Form` reads into the makers' plain data, by the rules a text widget is read by, and its appearances made: a toggle's under the on state's name its group gives it (`group::on_state_name`, the widget's index only where `/Opt` has an entry for it, as `Field::group` names it), its mark in the default resources' ZapfDingbats where that is written by name, else in ZapfDingbats by name; a push button's in its `/DA` font, at 12 points where it has no `/DA`. `appearance::Unmade::NotButton` for a field of another kind, and `appearance::Unmade::Icon` for a push button that shows an icon, which isn't drawn.
- `Limits`, `Cap`, `Error`, and `Fault`: caps on the fields and widgets read, the tree's depth, reference hops, and a value's items; a field reached twice is left out, so a cycle through `/Kids` ends.
- `appearance::FieldFont` and `appearance::FieldLayoutFont` (`text-layout`): the font a field's text is drawn in, found by its `/DA` name in the default resources and read with `pdf-fonts` (`FieldFont::find`, `Form::field_font`, or `FieldFont::find_in_catalog`, which reads the default resources from the catalog and nothing else of the form), or a standard 14 font that name falls back to (`FieldFont::standard`), with the resource an appearance names it by (`FieldFont::res`), or the dictionary a caller writes as an object where none can name it (`FieldFont::direct`), and, for its layout, a descriptor's `/Ascent` and `/Descent` as given where it has a `/FontBBox`, past the box or not (`pdf_fonts::Font::descriptor_heights`), and the heights of the face PDFium (as of chromium/8152) draws a standard 14 font in where nothing gives them, as PDFium measures a field's lines, and no box for a standard 14 Type 1 font that embeds no program.
- `appearance::text`, `appearance::TextField`, `appearance::WidgetLook`, `appearance::Appearance`, and `appearance::Unmade` (`text-layout`): a text field's appearance made from plain data as PDFium (as of chromium/8152) makes it, its background, border in each of the five styles, a comb field's dividers, `/Tx` sequence with its clip, and text (a single line, wrapped lines, comb cells, a password, an automatic size from PDFium's sizes), turned by `/MK /R` through its box and matrix, or why none could be made (a cap passed, no box, a number that isn't finite, the box inside the border among them, a size or box that sets the text further out than `1e35`, which a reader working in `f32` may not place, or a character the font can't draw, never a `?` drawn). The text's color is `/DA`'s nonstroking one alone, a size below `0.0001` is an automatic one, as PDFium takes it, and a color's components are written within `0..=1`. The font is any `pdf_gfx::FontSet`, one font or several, each grapheme cluster drawn in the first that has it; a font of a set past the first with no resource, where something is drawn in it, is `Unmade::FontNotBindable`.
- Text cut into extended grapheme clusters and drawn composed (`text-layout`): each cluster of a value (Unicode Standard Annex #29, by `unicode-segmentation`) in one font, composed (Normalization Form C, by `unicode-normalization`) where a font has the whole of it so, else as given, so `e` and a combining acute accent draw as `é` and a font that has a character's given form and not its composed one draws it as given; a cluster no font has is refused at the first of its characters, as given, that the first font can't draw, at its offset in the caller's text. A comb cell holds a cluster, and `/MaxLen` counts a comb field's clusters; a password's stars are one a character as given.
- `appearance::text_with_fallback`, `appearance::Fallback`, `appearance::MadeWithFallback`, and `WidgetText::make_with_fallback` (`embed` with `text-layout`): a value drawn in the field's font and a TrueType program the caller gives (`pdf_fonts::embed::Program`) for what that font can't draw, each cluster in the first that has it, the program's codes its glyphs' numbers in the whole program, two bytes each; with the program's glyphs drawn, and the characters they're drawn for (`pdf_fonts::embed::Glyphs`), gathered from what's drawn alone, for the caller to join across appearances and embed once. A value the field's font draws whole is drawn as `appearance::text` draws it, byte for byte, its glyphs none; a password's glyph is `*`'s, where the field's font has none, and no other.
- `appearance::widget_text`, `appearance::WidgetText`, `appearance::text_for_widget`, and `appearance::MadeText` (`text-layout`): a text widget's appearance from what `Form` reads, its `/DA` the widget's own else the field's, parsed once, and its font kept by the caller across widgets or read for one, with the name `/DA` gives the font (`MadeText::font_name`), which a fallback is added to the default resources under.
- `default_resources::font_name`: the name a writer puts a font object under in the form's `/DR /Font` for a `/DA` to name it by, the one wanted where it's free, `null` or that font's already, else the first number after it that is (`Helv1`), never another font's; it reads the dictionary alone, in time linear in it.
- `Limits::text` (the bytes of an appearance's text), `Limits::comb_cells` (a comb field's cells, 1,024 by default, which bound the dividers an empty field's appearance holds), `Limits::fonts`, and `Limits::font_budget`, with `Cap::Text`, `Cap::CombCells`, and `Cap::FontBudget`; `Fault::DefaultAppearance`, `Fault::MissingDefaultAppearance`, `Fault::FontNotInResources`, and `Fault::Font`.
- Features `programs` and `text-layout` (default), `predefined-cmaps`, `embed`, and `tracing`, each forwarded to the crates beneath; `text-layout` adds the `appearance` module, with `unicode-normalization` and `unicode-segmentation`, and `embed` (which brings in `subsetter` through `pdf-fonts`, and doesn't turn `text-layout` on) adds `appearance::text_with_fallback` beside it.

### Notes

- A text field's appearance is laid out as PDFium (as of chromium/8152) lays it out, its rules learnt from PDFium's sources (BSD-3-Clause) with no code copied, and held to the appearances PDFium makes for the same fields, recorded as cases. Where it differs, ISO 32000-2's or Acrobat's way is kept, as the README lists: the clip always written, an empty `/Tx BMC EMC`, `/MaxLen` cutting only comb text, a missing font's fallback by its name, the border read from `/BS` over `/Border` at its fractional width and dashed as given, a negative `/MK /R` clockwise, a beveled band over a CMYK background darkened, the last of two `Tf` or two colors in a `/DA`, a null `/V` inherited, decomposed text drawn composed, and a comb cell a grapheme cluster. PDFium draws in no font a caller gives: what's drawn with a fallback is held to PDFium's text extraction of it, each character where it's drawn.
- Re-exports `pdf-objects`, `pdf-content-streams`, `pdf-semantics`, `pdf-fonts`, `pdf-gfx`, and `pdf-annots` at the crate root. Requires Rust 1.92.
- Never panics on untrusted input, and `Debug` implementations and `tracing` spans never include a field's name or value, a font's name, or an appearance's content; the one exception is `appearance::Unmade::Unencodable`, which names the one character a font couldn't draw, and never one of a password's.
