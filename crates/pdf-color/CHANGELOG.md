# Changelog

## 0.1.0-alpha.1 (unreleased)

- **Components as PDFium counts them.** `components`, over any resolver, as of chromium/8125, of the value a `/ColorSpace` dictionary holds under a name, as written, or an inline image's array: `Some(n)`, 3 where PDFium loads no space, or none for a reference to nothing, for which it has no space at all. `counted` gives the same as a `Counted`, with whether a cap ended a branch of it. A device name, its abbreviation or `Pattern` by name, and no other family; an array of one by what it holds; an array of more by the first four bytes of its family, a name's or a string's, each family as PDFium loads it; an alternate space by a path of its own, which also takes a stream, by the first of its values in the order of its keys that names a stock space, and doesn't go into an array of one. A Pattern space counts its underlying space's and the pattern's name, up to 16. References are followed as far as PDFium does, and a loop doesn't load.
- **Ids are pdf-objects' struct.** `Place::object` and `Fault::Damaged { id }` hold pdf-objects' `ObjectId`, a struct of `number` and `generation`, where it was the pair `(u32, u16)`: `(1, 0)` is `ObjectId::new(1, 0)`, `id.0` is `id.number`.
- **`Place` and `Sink` are pdf-objects'.** A `Fault` is told to pdf-objects' `fault::Sink<Fault>` with its `fault::Place`, the types pdf-semantics' and pdf-fonts' faults are told to, so one sink can take all three; `Place` and `Sink` are no longer this crate's, nor at its root. pdf-objects is re-exported whole (`pdf_color::pdf_objects`), so a sink of one's own names them at the version this crate takes. Code follows: `use pdf_color::{Place, Sink}` is `use pdf_objects::fault::{Place, Sink}`, or `pdf_color::pdf_objects::fault`, `impl Sink for S` is `impl Sink<pdf_color::Fault> for S`, a bound `S: Sink` is `S: Sink<Fault>` and `&mut dyn Sink` is `&mut dyn Sink<Fault>`; a `Vec<(Place, Fault)>` keeps them as before.
- **Numbers as PDFium's accessors read them.** `/N`, `/FunctionType`, `/BitsPerSample` and `/Size`'s elements take a real cut from its 32-bit float, `true` as 1, an integer past 32 bits as 0 and anything else as 0; a white point's take a number's 32-bit float, and `Y` is compared as one.
- **A function's shape, by its dictionary alone.** What a DeviceN space asks of its tint transform: how many inputs and outputs it has, or that PDFium refuses it, for each of the four kinds, no stream decoded and nothing evaluated. So a sampled function shorter than its sizes say and a PostScript function that doesn't parse count as loading, where PDFium's don't: `tests/pdfium/differ.tsv` holds them to it.
- **The spaces, typed, as ISO 32000-2's 8.6 says them.** `Space::read`, over any resolver: `Family`, `Space` and its `#[non_exhaustive]` structs `Cie`, `Icc`, `Indexed`, `Separation`, `DeviceN`, `Attributes` and `Process`, `Lookup` and `Subtype`, with `Space::family`, `components`, `base`, `alternate` and `colorants`. `Icc::id` and `Lookup::Stream`'s `id` are a stream's number, what a reader asks for its data by. All of it borrows from the file. No conversion, no profile opened, no function evaluated, no stream decoded. A name that's no family is no space: `Spaces` is what looks one up.
- **Colorants.** `Colorants`, an iterator over a Separation space's one or a DeviceN space's names, each through its references, and `Colorant::of`, what a name says of itself: `Cyan`, `Magenta`, `Yellow`, `Black`, `All`, `None` or `Spot`. `Attributes::separations` reads a `/Colorants` dictionary's spaces on asking, each a `ColorantSpace`. `DeviceN::names` and `Process::components` hold each element by its position, none for one that's no name.
- **`Fault` and `Sink`.** Each departure, a `thiserror` value with its clause, told to a sink with its place (pdf-objects' `fault::Sink` and `fault::Place`, above); a `Vec` keeps them, and `()` wants none. Reading goes on past each. What only a check reads is behind `Sink::wants`, and spends a budget of its own, so the same space is given whatever the sink, an `Err` included, `()` pays for no check, and a read a check makes is never the `Err`: damaged, it's told, and any other failure leaves the check unsaid. References are followed through, each lookup a visit, a chain told as `Chained`, a loop as `Loop`, the caps as `TooDeep` and `TooManyVisits`.
- **One promise between the two readings.** A space read with a sink that wants faults, none told, counts by `Space::components` as `components` counts it, where `counted` meets no cap, but for a Pattern space over more than 16 components, which no clause refuses and PDFium does: the fuzz target and every case in `tests/pdfium` hold it. So a white point above 0 that PDFium's 32-bit reading makes 0 is told, past ISO 32000-2.
- **What a resources dictionary names.** `Spaces::read` finds its `/ColorSpace` dictionary, a stream's among them, `/Resources` and `/ColorSpace` each through at most two references, as PDFium: past that, or in a loop, none, and no error, a fault told of none of it. `Spaces::iter` and `Spaces::get` give each `Named` (`#[non_exhaustive]`): the key as the reader read it, where it was written and what it holds, a key written twice at its last entry and an empty key no name.
- **Caps.** `Limits` (`#[non_exhaustive]`), `Limits::DEFAULT`: how deep a space nests, 64, and how many objects are looked up for one, and subfunctions written in place read, 4,096 per space, and as many again for what a typed space's checks read, where each name kept and each `/Colorants` entry read spends one too. Past one, a branch doesn't load, so a chain of arrays of one by reference past 64 counts otherwise than PDFium's, which has only its stack, and short of them a value nested in place past where PDFium's parser stops can too: `tests/pdfium/differ.tsv` holds each.
- **A failed read.** An object that's `Why::Damaged` isn't there, as for PDFium, and any other failure to read one is the `Err`, whole, through `components` and `Spaces::read` alike.
- **Benchmarks.** `cargo bench -p pdf-color` times the count, the typed reading and a resources dictionary's spaces, on inputs the benchmarks make as they run, the same on every run: `counted`, and `Space::read` with `()` and with a sink that keeps every fault, of a space of each kind a resources dictionary or an inline image holds (DeviceGray by name, DeviceRGB in an array of one, CalRGB, ICCBased, Indexed, Separation, an NChannel DeviceN space with its attributes, Pattern over DeviceRGB, and DeviceN whose tint transform stitches eight functions by reference); a `/ColorSpace` dictionary of 8, 64 and 1,024 names found by `Spaces::read`, each name counted, as a reader of content counts a page's; and a DeviceN space's 32 colorants, each by `Colorant::of`. Each input is held to what it gives before it's timed: a count to PDFium's, with no cap met; a typed space to its family, its components and no fault; a dictionary to each name's count, in order; the colorants to their tally. Throughput is in spaces, or in names or colorants. So a change is judged before and after it. The harness is criterion, a dev-dependency never built for a user of the crate, and `benches/` is outside the package.
- **The package, checked before a publish.** CI packages the crate as a publish would (`cargo publish --dry-run`, in a target directory of its own made fresh each time, so a stale build can't pass or fail it) and checks what the package holds: `src/`, the README, the CHANGELOG, the manifest and the lock, nothing else, no benchmark among them. The manifest names the crate's documentation (docs.rs). The tests read files through pdf-structure, which isn't published: it's a dev-dependency by its path alone, which the package's manifest leaves out, so the crate packages and publishes on pdf-objects alone, and its own unit tests don't build from the package.
- `Dict`, `Object`, `ObjectId`, `Resolve`, `Span`, `Str`, `Stream`, `Unreadable`, `Value`, `Why` and `dict!` are pdf-objects', re-exported.
