# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

- **Components as PDFium counts them.** `components`, over any resolver, as of chromium/8125, of the value a `/ColorSpace` dictionary holds under a name, as written, or an inline image's array: `Some(n)`, 3 where PDFium loads no space, or none for a reference to nothing, for which it has no space at all. A device name, its abbreviation or `Pattern` by name, and no other family; an array of one by what it holds; an array of more by the first four bytes of its family, a name's or a string's, each family as PDFium loads it; an alternate space by a path of its own, which also takes a stream, by the first of its values in the order of its keys that names a stock space, and doesn't go into an array of one. A Pattern space counts its underlying space's and the pattern's name, up to 16. References are followed as far as PDFium does, and a loop doesn't load.
- **Numbers as PDFium's accessors read them.** `/N`, `/FunctionType`, `/BitsPerSample` and `/Size`'s elements take a real cut from its 32-bit float, `true` as 1, an integer past 32 bits as 0 and anything else as 0; a white point's take a number's 32-bit float, and `Y` is compared as one.
- **A function's shape, by its dictionary alone.** What a DeviceN space asks of its tint transform: how many inputs and outputs it has, or that PDFium refuses it, for each of the four kinds, no stream decoded and nothing evaluated. So a sampled function shorter than its sizes say and a PostScript function that doesn't parse count as loading, where PDFium's don't: `tests/pdfium/differ.tsv` holds them to it.
- **What a resources dictionary names.** `Spaces::read` finds its `/ColorSpace` dictionary, a stream's among them, `/Resources` and `/ColorSpace` each through at most two references, as PDFium: past that, or in a loop, none, and no error. `Spaces::iter` and `Spaces::get` give each `Named` (`#[non_exhaustive]`): the key as the reader read it, where it was written and what it holds, a key written twice at its last entry and an empty key no name.
- **Caps.** `Limits` (`#[non_exhaustive]`), `Limits::DEFAULT`: how deep a space nests, 64, and how many objects are looked up for one, and subfunctions written in place read, 4,096 per space. Past one, a branch doesn't load, so a chain of arrays of one by reference past 64 counts otherwise than PDFium's, which has only its stack.
- **A failed read.** An object that's `Why::Damaged` isn't there, as for PDFium, and any other failure to read one is the `Err`, whole, through `components` and `Spaces::read` alike.
- `Dict`, `Object`, `ObjectId`, `Resolve`, `Span`, `Str`, `Stream`, `Unreadable`, `Value` and `Why` are pdf-objects', re-exported.
