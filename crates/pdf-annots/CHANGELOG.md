# Changelog

## 0.1.0-alpha.1 (unreleased)

First release. Until `0.1.0` stabilizes, alpha releases may include breaking API changes documented here; depend on the exact version (`=0.1.0-alpha.1`).

### Added

- `Annot`, `page_annots`, `page_annots_with`, `page_tabs`, `TabOrder`, and `MarkupInfo`: zero-I/O page `/Annots` traversal and `&Dict` wrapper under `Rules::Iso` and `Rules::Pdfium` (as of chromium/8152).
- `AnnotSubtype` and `TypedAnnot`: typed views for `Text`, `Link`, `FreeText`, `Highlight`/`Underline`/`Squiggly`/`StrikeOut` (`TextMarkupAnnot`, `QuadPoints`), `Line`, `Square`/`Circle` (`ShapeAnnot`), `Polygon`/`PolyLine` (`PolyAnnot`), `Ink`, `Stamp`, `Caret`, `Popup`, `FileAttachment`, `Redact`, and `Widget`.
- `LineAnnot::intent` (`LineIntent`) and `PolyAnnot::intent` (`PolyIntent`): a `/Line`'s and a `/Polygon`'s or `/PolyLine`'s `/IT` (ISO 32000-2 Table 178 and Table 181), so a reader tells an arrow, a cloud, and a dimension apart (a dimension's `/Measure` is left to the dictionary); `None` where `/IT` is absent, and a name neither table has kept as written (`Other`).
- `AnnotFlags`, `AnnotColor`, `Border`, `BorderStyle`, `AppearanceChars`, `LineEnding`, and `Quadding`: common visual, border, and appearance-characteristic (`/MK`) parsing and serialization.
- `Border::to_box_border` and `From<BorderStyle> for pdf_gfx::BoxBorderStyle`: a `/BS` or legacy `/Border` border as `pdf-gfx`'s `BoxBorder`, for `Canvas::box_border` to draw around a rectangle.
- `DefaultAppearance`: `/DA` default-appearance parsing and serialization, re-exported from `pdf-gfx` (one type under both names): its color is a `pdf-content-streams` `Color` (`AnnotColor::to_content_color` converts one), it keeps the text-state scalars `Tc`, `Tw`, `Tz`, `TL`, `Tr`, and `Ts`, and `parse` takes `pdf-content-streams`' `Limits` (`Limits::content` here).
- `Appearances`, `AppearanceEntry`, `AppearanceMode`, `placement_matrix`, and `stream_placement_matrix`: `/AP` + `/AS` resolution and ISO 32000-2 §12.5.5 Form XObject placement matrices.
- Re-exports `Dest`, `ExplicitDest`, `PageTarget`, `DestFit`, `DestNameKind`, `DestTable`, `ResolvedDest`, `resolve_dest`, `Action`, `NamedAction`, `AdditionalActions`, and `OpenAction` from `pdf-semantics`.
- `AnnotThread`, `ReplyKind`, `MarkedState`, `ReviewState`, and `resolve_threads`: `/IRT` reply/group threading and effective state aggregation.
- `SynthesizedAp`, `synth_annot`, `synth_text_markup`, `synth_shape`, `synth_line`, `synth_poly`, `synth_ink`, `synth_stamp`, `synth_text_note`, `synth_free_text`, and `synth_redact`: deterministic `/AP /N` (and `/RO`) Form XObject synthesis over `pdf_gfx::Canvas`. `synth_free_text` and a plain `/Square` of `synth_shape` (no corner radius, no cloudy effect) draw their border in all five `/BS` styles through `Canvas::box_border`: solid, dashed, beveled, inset, and underline (ISO 32000-2 §12.5.4 Table 168), with FreeText's text padded to clear a bevel's two widths; a `/Circle` and a rounded or cloudy `/Square` stroke one path, solid or dashed. A plain `/Square`'s solid or dashed border over an interior color is one path too, filled and stroked together as a circle's is, so under an opacity the border shows its own color and not that color over the interior's. A dashed border with no dash array is drawn `[3]`, Table 168's default, wherever a border is stroked (lines, polygons, ink, and ellipses too).
- `SynthesizedAp::matrix`, `SynthesizedAp::needs`, and `SynthesizedAp::drawn(bbox, matrix, canvas)`: an appearance carries its form's `/Matrix` (the identity for every `synth_*` result) and what its content draws with by what each resource is (`pdf-content-streams`' `ResourceNeeds`), beside the finished `/Resources` dictionary, so a writer that shares resources across a file can bind them itself; `drawn` holds an appearance the caller drew on a `Canvas` (a custom stamp, say) the same way, its `resources` made from its needs as for the others, a resource already written bound by its number. `to_stream` writes `/Matrix` where it isn't the identity.
- `ApRect` and appearances drawn in place: `synth_text_markup`, `synth_shape`, `synth_line`, `synth_poly`, and `synth_ink` take an `impl Into<ApRect>` where they took the `/Rect` (a `Rect` is `ApRect::Form`, the form's own `[0 0 w h]` space, as before), and `ApRect::InPlace(rect)` draws the same shape where its geometry was given, in the page's default user space, the form's `/BBox` the `/Rect` itself. A form drawn in its own space has each coordinate given less the `/Rect`'s corner, so a renderer moving it back lands its edges a hair from where the same shape drawn on the page lands them, and its coverage can round the two apart: a writer whose appearances must match a drawing in the page's space to the pixel, or that keeps an appearance where its annotation is, draws in place. `synth_shape`'s `rect_diff` is in from `/Rect` either way.
- `RedactMark` and `synth_redact(rect, &RedactMark)`: a `/Redact` mark's `/AP /N` and `/RO` drawn over its `/QuadPoints`, each quadrilateral moved into the form's space: in `/AP /N` a translucent wash and an outline (inside the quadrilateral where it's a rectangle along the axes, along its edges otherwise), in `/RO` the interior color and the overlay text laid out, unrotated, in the quadrilateral's bounding box, repeated there under `/Repeat`. With no usable quadrilateral both cover the whole `/Rect`; a quadrilateral is drawn only as far as it lies inside `/Rect`, and one wholly outside it isn't usable. A mark with no interior color, or a transparent one, fills nothing in `/RO` (with `/IC` absent the region is left transparent), and its overlay text is then black unless `/DA` gives a color. `synth_annot` draws a `/Redact` it read over its `/QuadPoints` too. `RedactMark::bounds` is the quadrilaterals' bounding rectangle; its `Debug` gives the overlay text's length, never the text.
- `AnnotMut`, `BuiltAnnot`, and `push_page_annot`: in-place `&mut Dict` annotation mutators and pure typed annotation builders.
- Redaction marks authored whole (ISO 32000-2 §12.5.6.23): `AnnotMut::set_redact_overlay` (`/RO`), `set_overlay_text` (`/OverlayText`), `set_repeat` (`/Repeat`), and `set_rollover_appearance` (`/AP /R`, which leaves an `/AP` that isn't a direct dictionary as it is: `has_direct_appearance` tells); `BuiltAnnot::redact_mark(rect, &RedactMark)` and `BuiltAnnot::redact_quads(&RedactMark)`, which takes `/Rect` from the quadrilaterals, beside `BuiltAnnot::redact`. `BuiltAnnot::write_into` binds a redaction's overlay as `/AP /R` beside `/N`, so a viewer shows what applying the mark leaves while the pointer is over it. A built redaction's `/IC` is three `DeviceRGB` numbers whatever the color's space (none for a transparent color), and overlay text with no `/DA` given gets the one it was drawn with (`/Helv 0 Tf 1 g`, or `0 g` with no interior color), as the table asks for a `/DA` beside `/OverlayText`.
- `synth_free_text_with_font`, `synth_redact_with_font`, `BuiltAnnot::free_text_with_font`, and `BuiltAnnot::redact_with_font` (`text-layout` feature): a FreeText's text and a redaction's overlay text drawn in any `pdf_gfx::LayoutFont` under the font resource given, a font embedded in the document among them, where `synth_free_text`, `synth_redact`, and the other builders draw in Helvetica. A character the font can't draw is `Err(pdf_gfx::UnencodableChar)` with nothing built, where the Helvetica defaults leave the text out; the error holds that one character of the text. `/DA`'s font name isn't changed to the font given and no `/AcroForm /DR` is written: the appearance stream carries the font in its own `/Resources`.
- `FlattenTarget`, `FlattenOpts`, `FlattenPlan`, `plan_flatten`, and `plan_flatten_filtered`: zero-I/O page annotation flattening with `q ... Q` isolation and collision-free `/Resources/XObject` allocation. `/Redact` marks are kept on the page, neither drawn nor removed, and each mark's `/Popup` with it, unless `FlattenOpts::include_redactions` (`with_redactions(true)`) asks for them: flattening leaves the content under a mark in the file, so a mark painted onto the page would only look like a redaction. PDFium's `FPDFPage_Flatten` (as of chromium/8152) flattens them; the default here departs from it on purpose.
- `Limits`, `Cap`, `Error`, and `Fault`: deterministic resource caps and non-fatal diagnostics reported via `pdf-objects`' `fault::Sink`.

### Notes

- Re-exports `pdf-objects`, `pdf-content-streams`, `pdf-semantics`, `pdf-fonts`, and `pdf-gfx` at the crate root. Requires Rust 1.92.
- A FreeText whose `/DA` gives a `Tf` size of exactly `0` is sized to its box under `text-layout` (between 4 and 12, 12 itself where the text fits at it, as `DefaultAppearance::text_box_style` maps it), and drawn at 12 without the feature. With no `/DA`, no `Tf`, or a size below 0 the text is drawn at 12 either way. A redaction's overlay text keeps its own default, from its region's height. Of `/DA`'s operators only `Tf`'s size and the nonstroking color (`g`, `rg`, `k`) are applied when an appearance is synthesized, and a stroking color (`G`, `RG`, `K`) never colors the text, which is filled: `Tc`, `Tw`, `Tz`, `TL`, `Tr`, and `Ts` are read and written back, so a `/DA` saying `3 Tr` still draws filled text.
- Never panics on untrusted input, and `Debug` implementations and `tracing` spans never include file plaintext, URIs, destination names, or annotation text; the one exception is `pdf_gfx::UnencodableChar`, the `*_with_font` functions' error, which names the one character a font couldn't draw.
