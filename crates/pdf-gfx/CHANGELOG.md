# Changelog

## 0.1.0-alpha.1 (unreleased)

First release. Until `0.1.0` stabilizes, alpha releases may include breaking API changes documented here; depend on the exact version (`=0.1.0-alpha.1`).

### Added

- `Point`, `Rect`, `Quad`, `Matrix`, `Rotation`, `PageSize`, and `PageGeom`: 2D PDF geometry, affine matrix composition and inversion, axis-aligned rectangle placement (`Matrix::onto`), and closed-form transforms between PDF PageSpace and viewer ViewSpace (`PageGeom::to_view`, `PageGeom::from_view`, `PageGeom::from_page`).
- `interpret` and `interpret_with`: bounded zero-I/O graphics and text state interpreter over `Concat` and `Resolve + Streams`, matching PDFium's content interpretation rules as of chromium/8152 (`Forms::Descend` and `Forms::Skip`, `Opts`, `Wants`, `Summary`).
- `State` and `TextState`: thread-safe (`Send + Sync`) graphics state tracking across `q`/`Q`/`cm`, line style, `DashPattern`, `RenderingIntent`, `ActiveColorSpace`, `ColorValues`, `BlendState`, `SoftMask`, and `ExtGState` (`gs`) dictionaries.
- `PathDrawn`, `Seg`, `Clip`, `ClipPart`, and `ClipParts`: path construction, fill/stroke bounds, `W`/`W*` path clips (including PDFium's single-point clip and auto-merge rules), text rendering modes `4..=7` (`Tr`) clipping, and Form XObject `/BBox` clips.
- `TextShow`, `Glyphs`, and `Glyph`: lazy glyph placement over `pdf-fonts` across `Tj`, `TJ`, `'`, and `"`, with horizontal and vertical writing modes, `char_box` outline union, pen anchors, glyph matrices (`Glyph::tm`), and decoded Unicode text (`Glyph::text`).
- `FormUse`, `UseId`, `Refused`, `ImageDrawn`, `ImageOf`, and `ShadingDrawn`: nested Form XObject entry/exit/refusal events, placed XObject and inline image metadata with effective horizontal/vertical DPI (`ImageDrawn::dpi`, `ImageDrawn::dpi_with_unit`), and `sh` shading bounds.
- `Mark`, `MarkProps`, `Marks`, and `MarksIter`: marked-content sequence (`BMC`/`BDC`/`EMC`) and point (`MP`/`DP`) tracking across Form XObject boundaries with `/MCID`, `/ActualText`, `/OC`, and `/Artifact` helpers.
- `encode`, `encode_with`, and `EncodedPage`: content-stream and Form XObject re-encoder that rewrites relative line-moving text operators (`Td`, `TD`, `T*`, `'`, `"`) into position-independent `Tm` + `Td` pairs while preserving exact glyph, path, clip, and mark geometry.
- `Canvas` and `TextPiece`: high-level vector, inset border, rounded-rectangle, circle/ellipse, clip, placed image/Form XObject, marked-content, and `TJ`-kerned text builder over `ContentWriter`.
- `BoxBorderStyle`, `BoxBorder`, and `Canvas::box_border`: a rectangle's border drawn strictly inside it, in one balanced `q`..`Q` scope, in the five border styles of ISO 32000-2 §12.5.4 Table 168: `Solid` and `Dashed` (the frame inset by half the width; `[3]` where the dash array is empty, negative, non-finite, or all zeros), `Underline` (one segment along the bottom), and `Beveled` and `Inset` (the frame, then two mitered bands of the border's width inside it, shaded light and dark from an optional fill hint). `BoxBorder` borrows its dash array and is `Copy`; `BoxBorder::inset` says how far the border reaches into its box (twice the width for the two bevels). `Canvas::box_bands` draws a beveled or inset border's two bands alone, without the frame, in a scope of their own: the same operators `box_border` writes inside its frame, for a box whose edge is shaded where no color strokes its frame, and nothing for the other styles or where `box_border` would draw no bands. Every style sets its own dash inside its scope (a solid line for all but `Dashed`), so a dash the caller left set never shows in a border; the line cap, join and miter limit are the caller's. Nothing is drawn for a width that isn't finite and above `0`, a box that isn't finite (or that, grown by five times the width, isn't: a stroke whose extent `interpret` couldn't bound), or a box too small for the style.
- `DefaultAppearance`: bounded `/DA` default-appearance parsing (`parse`, under `pdf-content-streams`' `Limits`) and deterministic serialization (`to_bytes`) of the font resource name and size (`Tf`), device color (`g`/`G`, `rg`/`RG`, `k`/`K` as a `pdf-content-streams` `Color`), and the text-state scalars `Tc`, `Tw`, `Tz`, `TL`, `Tr`, and `Ts` (ISO 32000-2 §12.7.4.3), with `size` (the fixed size a `Tf` above `0` gives), `auto_sized` (a `Tf` size of exactly `0`) and, under `text-layout`, `text_box_style` (auto-sized for exactly `0`; the default style's `12` where `Tf` is absent or its size is negative). `to_bytes` writes a color's components clamped to `0..=1`, as `parse` reads them, leaves out a size that has no font name, and writes `/Helv` for a name holding a NUL byte, which no name can. `parse_with` reads the same, whatever its `fault::Sink<Fault>`, and tells it what departs from §12.7.4.3, each at its byte offset into the string: `Fault::DefaultAppearanceUnreadable` (a string that fails to lex or passes a cap, its `pdf-content-streams` error's kind telling which; `None` is returned), `Fault::DefaultAppearanceOperand` (an operator ignored, or `Tf` read in part, for an operand missing or not finite, or a font that isn't a name), `Fault::DefaultAppearanceTextMode` (a `Tr` outside `0..=7`), and `Fault::DefaultAppearanceNoFont` (no `Tf` names a font); a sink that wants none, `&mut ()` among them, is told nothing, and `parse` is `parse_with` with `&mut ()`.
- `TextPage`, `TextPageOpts`, `PageChar`, `TextLine`, `SearchOpts`, and `TextMatch` (`text-layout` feature): `CPDF_TextPage`-compatible reading-order assembly with synthetic spaces/newlines, faux-bold deduplication, `/ActualText` replacement, `unicode-bidi` RTL reordering, soft-hyphen wrap detection, hit-testing, rectangular text extraction, merged selection quads, and search.
- `layout_text`, `Canvas::draw_layout`, `Canvas::text_box`, `LayoutFont`, `FontEncoder`, `TextBoxStyle`, `TextAlign`, `VerticalAlign`, `WrapMode`, `LaidOutText`, `LaidOutLine`, and `UnencodableChar` (`text-layout` feature): word/character wrapping, alignment, justification, automatic font sizing (the maximum size itself where the text fits at it), and comb-cell layout over `Std14` and `Font`. Two `TextBoxStyle` flags, both off by default, where off lays out as before: `line_end_spaces` keeps the spaces a wrapped line is broken at, finding words as PDFium's line breaking (as of chromium/8152) finds them (a line breaks before the last word begun on it where its next character, a space among them, would pass the box's width, so the spaces before that word end the line, in its codes, its range and the width it's aligned by; no space is trimmed or passed over; a word begins after a space or punctuation that doesn't join, an apostrophe, comma, hyphen, full stop and solidus joining, at an opening bracket, at a currency sign, and at or after an ideograph), and `font_heights_as_given` measures lines by the font's ascent and descent as `LayoutFont::font_metrics` gives them, without the floors (`0.70` and `-0.15` em, a height of `0.85`) that otherwise apply, which still do where the heights aren't finite or the ascent isn't above the descent.
- `Limits`, `Cap`, `Error`, `Unwritable`, and `Fault`: per-page interpretation budgets and non-fatal diagnostics reported via `pdf-objects`' `fault::Sink`.
- Cargo features `programs` (default, forwarding `pdf-fonts/programs`), `text-layout` (default, enabling `TextPage` and multiline `Canvas` layout), `predefined-cmaps` (forwarding `pdf-fonts/predefined-cmaps`), and `tracing` (numeric phase spans).

### Notes

- Re-exports `pdf-objects`, `pdf-color`, `pdf-content-streams`, `pdf-semantics`, and `pdf-fonts` at the crate root. Requires Rust 1.92.
- Never panics on untrusted input, and `Debug` implementations and `tracing` spans never include file plaintext, resource names, tag names, or stream bytes.
- `Point`, `Rect`, `Quad`, `Matrix`, `Rotation`, `Seg`, `Forms`, `MarkProps`, and `TextPiece` are exhaustive on purpose; other public enums and configuration/metadata structs are `#[non_exhaustive]` so new variants and fields can be added without breaking callers. `TextBoxStyle` is exhaustive too, so that it can be built with a struct literal; build it with `..TextBoxStyle::default()` after the fields set, as a field added later breaks only a literal that leaves that out.
