# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

- **Tokens.** `tokens(bytes, &ResCx, &Limits)` gives a content stream's tokens in order, a `Tokens` iterator (fused, `Clone`, `Send` and `Sync`) of each token's range and its `Tok`: `Op`, an operator, any word that isn't a number, `true`, `false` or `null`; `Operand`, an operand's bytes as written, a number, a name, a string, an array or a dictionary with all it holds, or a closing delimiter with nothing to close; or `InlineImage`, `BI` to `EI`, one token, with its data's range. Between them is only white-space and comments, and after an error nothing comes. Tokens end where PDFium's `CPDF_StreamParser` ends them: at white-space and delimiters, a word of digits, signs and points a number, strings nested, and an array or a dictionary one token.
- **Inline images** ended by PDFium's `ReadInlineStream` rules, by the first filter: unfiltered, by the data's exact size (`/W`, `/H`, `/BPC` and its color space's components, rows padded to a byte; an image mask's one bit of one component; a name the resources don't have read as none); `/AHx` to its `>`, `/A85` past the first byte that's none of its digits or white-space, and a `>` after it, and `/RL` to its end-of-data byte, as PDFium's decoders end them; then on to the next `EI`, its tokens read as PDFium reads them. A filter PDFium can't decode, one of those that finds no end, or an image no size counts reads on from `ID` to the next `EI`. `/L` and `/Length` aren't read, as PDFium reads neither. `/Fl`, `/LZW`, `/CCF` and `/DCT`, which PDFium decodes to find their end, aren't decoded: a JPEG ends at its end of image, the others, or a JPEG with none, at the first `EI` with white-space before it and white-space, a delimiter or the end after. The dictionary is read as PDFium reads it: a key matched as written, its `#` escapes not decoded; a value's decoded, and an abbreviated name read as the full one, a color space looked up by it (`/I` as `/Indexed`); of a key written both abbreviated and in full, the abbreviation's value, unless the full name's is an abbreviated name, which PDFium sets over it; the filter a name, or a string's bytes, naming one, and `null`, a dictionary or an empty array none. A JPEG's end of image is searched for once for the images that share it, or that find none. `ResCx` gives the components of the color spaces the resources name (`ResCx::new`, `ResCx::none`), and `family_components` counts a family as the lexer does.
- **Operations.** `operations(bytes, &ResCx, &Limits)` and `Concat::operations` give a content stream's operations in order, an `Operations` iterator (fused, `Clone`, `Send` and `Sync`) of `Operation`s, its tokens grouped: each operator, or inline image, with the operands since the last, and after an error nothing. An operation's operands' ranges are held in the item (tinyvec's `TinyVec`), sixteen inline, as many as PDFium keeps, so an operation of no more allocates nothing; more spill to the heap, every operand kept up to `Limits::operands`, past which is the operand cap's error, where the first past it starts. Operands at the end, which no operator takes, are no operation. `Operation`: `name`, the operator as written (`BI` for an inline image); `at`, where the operator is, or the image, `BI` to `EI`; `range`, from its first operand; `operands`, in order, an exact-size iterator; `arg(i)`, operand `i` from the last, as PDFium takes them, none past the first for any `i`; and `inline_image`. Its `Debug` shows the operation, not the content.
- **Inline images as operations.** `InlineImage`: `at`, `dict` (what's between `BI` and `ID`), `data`, `entry(key)`, the value under a key, as written, as PDFium reads the dictionary (either spelling of a key 8.9.7 abbreviates finding the one value; a key matched as written; of a key written twice, the last); and `length`, its `/L` or `/Length` where that's a length, `/L`'s where both are written.
- **Operands as written.** `Operand`, an operand's bytes and where they are (`bytes`, `range`), `Copy`: `name` and `object` read it as the functions of those names do, and `object_with` with a sink; `elements`, an array's elements as written, each an `Operand` where it is, found as they're asked for (`Elements`), none for what isn't an array; and what kind it is: `is_number` (a word of digits, signs and points, as PDFium tells a number), `is_name`, `is_string`, `is_array`, `is_dict`, from how it starts.
- **Operands.** `name` decodes a name operand's `#` escapes, borrowing it when there are none, a `#` without two hex digits kept as written; `object(operand, &Limits)` reads an operand as a pdf-objects `Object`, unplaced: an integer or a real by Rust's reading of the word, one past 64 bits as pdf-objects reads it (the nearest integer that fits, the largest finite real of its sign), `true`, `false`, `null`, a name decoded, a string's bytes in its `Form`, and arrays and dictionaries read in one pass, a dictionary keeping every entry, as `Dict` does, and passing over an entry whose key isn't a name; none for what isn't an object, one nested deeper than `Limits::depth`, or one of more values than `Limits::values`. It reads a token's bytes as well as an `Operand`'s. pdf-objects is re-exported (`pdf_content_streams::pdf_objects`).
- **A page as one.** `Concat` joins streams with a space between each, as PDFium joins a page's, one stream borrowed as it is: `bytes`, `stream_count`, `stream`, `range`, `pos` and `offset` (a joined offset to a `Pos`, a stream and a place in it, and back), `span` and `offsets` (a range to a `SpanAcross` and back), and `tokens`. `Join` joins them as they're read, under `Limits::content`, and under 4 GiB whatever it says, past which a `Pos` can't count: `push`, `push_with`, which hands a decoder the joined bytes to append to and the room left (`room`), cutting back to where they were, the space too, when it fails or writes past its room, and `finish`. A stream pushed, an empty one too, is the next stream, so a `Pos`'s stream is the index of the stream given. Past the cap is `TooLarge`; `push_with`'s refusal is `Pushed`, the cap's or the decoder's error.
- **Caps and errors.** `Limits` (`#[non_exhaustive]`, set on a copy of `Limits::DEFAULT`): `operands` before an operator (65,536), `depth` of arrays and dictionaries (512, PDFium's; reading an operand nests on the stack a level for each, so a far higher cap needs a stack to match), `values` an operand read as an object may hold (2^25, as pdf-objects'), `inline_image`'s data (64 MiB), and a page's `content` joined (256 MiB). `Error`, an `ErrorKind` and the offset where what's wrong starts, displayed as the kind and its byte: a string, hex string, array, dictionary or inline image that doesn't end, nesting past the cap, operands past it, an inline image's data past it; and `Marked` and `Emc`, for marked content that doesn't end and an `EMC` that ends none. Derived with thiserror; `ErrorKind` is `#[non_exhaustive]`. Never a panic, whatever the bytes.
- **Faults.** `fault::Sink` and `fault::Fault` (`#[non_exhaustive]`, thiserror, borrowing the content read): where ISO 32000-2 would read content otherwise than it's read here, by PDFium's rules, told with where it is, each fault's doc naming its clause and whether it's a "shall": `Number`, a word of digits, signs and points that ISO doesn't write as a number; `OutOfRange`, an integer outside 64 bits or a real past a 64-bit float's, each read as the nearest that fits; `HexString`, a byte that's neither a hex digit nor white-space; `Name`, a `#` without two hex digits after it, or `#00`; `Closer`, a `)`, `>`, `]` or `>>` with nothing to close; `Brace`, a `{` or `}`; `Keyword`, `Key` and `NoValue`, inside an array, a dictionary or an inline image's dictionary, a word that's no object, a key that's an object but no name, and a key with no value; `Across`, a string, at any depth in an array or a dictionary, an inline image's data, or a comment that reads a token of the next stream's first line as comment, running from one of a page's streams into the next, where ISO lets them divide only between its lexical tokens (an array, a dictionary or an inline image's dictionary divided between its tokens reads alike, and isn't told); `Trailing`, operands at the end that no operator takes; and an inline image's `EscapedKey`, a key with a `#` escape that ISO decodes to one it reads and PDFium matches as written, `Abbreviation`, a key written both abbreviated and in full where PDFium reads the full name's abbreviated value and ISO gives the abbreviation precedence, `ImageLength`, its `/L` or `/Length` ending its data elsewhere than it's found, `AfterId`, its `ID` with no white-space after it where the last of its filters isn't one of the two ASCII ones, and `BeforeEi`, tokens between its data and its `EI`. `Tokens::with_sink` and `Operations::with_sink` tell a token's own, an inline image's, and over a `Concat` (`Concat::tokens`, `Concat::operations`) a page's joins'; `Operations` also the operands at the end; `Operand::object_with` what's inside an array or a dictionary, as it's read. `Tokens` and `Operations` take the sink as a type parameter, `()` unless one's given, whose `wants()` is false, so none is looked for; a sink borrowed, `&mut S` or `&mut dyn Sink`, is one. What's read is the same through any sink, a sink that doesn't want faults is told none, and faults come in the order of their ends.
- **Operators.** `Operator` (`#[non_exhaustive]`), each of the 73 operators of ISO 32000-2's Annex A by what it does, and `Other` for any other word: `ALL`, the 73; `of`, the operator a name names, as written; `name`; `operands`, the count and types of its operands as the clause defining it gives them (`Operands`: `Fixed` types, or `Components` for `SC` and `sc`, a number for each component of a color space of one, three or four, or `ComponentsOrPattern` for `SCN` and `scn`, the same in any space or a pattern's name, one at least and no most; `Type`: a number, an integer, a name, a string, an array, or a name or a dictionary); `category`, its category of 8.2 (`Category`, sixteen, each with the clause that defines its operators); `clause`; `paint`, how a path-painting operator paints (`Paint`, with `fills` and `strokes`), `F` as `f`; `clip`, the rule `W` or `W*` clips by (`FillRule`); and `builds_path`. `Operation::operator` types an operation, an inline image's `BI`. `Operators`, a set of operators, `Other` among them: `NONE`, `ALL`, a const for each category, `of` (names), `contains`, `with`, `without`, `union`, `intersection`, `difference`, `is_empty`, `len`, `iter`, `FromIterator`, and a `Debug` of their names. `State` (`#[non_exhaustive]`), where content is among the graphics objects of 8.2 (Figure 9, with the errata: a text object allows the general graphics state, `q` and `Q` among it, and marked content): `ContentStream`, `Path`, `ClippingPath`, `Text`, `InlineImage`, and `GlyphStart`, the start of a Type 3 glyph description, where `d0` or `d1` comes first, allowed nowhere else; `allows`, `allowed`, `after` (the state an operator leaves, where it's allowed; `sh` and `Do` back at once) and `step` (an operation's, an inline image `BI`, `ID` and `EI` in turn, so it ends where it began). The table's lookups are `const fn`s; `Other` has no operands, category or clause, and is allowed in no state.
- **Balance, marked content and names.** `balance(&Concat, &ResCx, &Limits)` counts a page's `q`s and `Q`s as one, its streams joined: `Balance` (`#[non_exhaustive]`), `net`, the `q`s less the `Q`s, and `min_prefix`, the lowest the running count goes from 0. `marked(&Concat, &Properties, &ResCx, &Limits)` finds its marked content, each range in the order it opens (`Marked`, `#[non_exhaustive]`): `tag`, the name before its `BMC` or `BDC`, decoded, empty when that isn't a name; `props`, a `BDC`'s list (`Props`: `Inline`, a dictionary in the content, as pdf-objects' `Dict`, or `Named`, the name and the list `Properties` has under it, if any, borrowed from it, so ranges naming one list share it; `dict` either way); `span`, from its operator's first operand through its `EMC`, across the streams; and `depth`, the ranges it's inside. `Properties::new` takes the property lists the caller read from the resources' `/Properties`, each under its decoded name (`get`, `lists`). An `EMC` with none open is `ErrorKind::Emc` where it is, a range still open at the end `ErrorKind::Marked` where the first of those starts, and one opened past `Limits::depth` `ErrorKind::Depth`. `names_used(&Concat, &ResCx, &Limits, except)` gives the resource names content uses, decoded, but for those only operations inside the ranges `except` use (`NameSet`: `contains`, `of`, `iter`, `len`, `is_empty`, `remove`), by category (`ResKind`, `#[non_exhaustive]`, `ALL`, a slice, and `key`): `Tf`'s font, `Do`'s external object, `gs`'s graphics state, `sh`'s shading, `cs`'s and `CS`'s color space and an inline image's, but for the device spaces and `/Pattern`, an image's by the name PDFium looks it up by, its abbreviation read as the full name, `scn`'s and `SCN`'s pattern where the last operand is a name, and `BDC`'s and `DP`'s property list where it's named. `kept(&Concat, &ResCx, &Limits, drop)` reads in one pass what content keeps once the ranges `drop` are taken out (`Kept`, `#[non_exhaustive]`: its `balance`, its `names`, and the names only what's taken out uses, `dropped`), an operation taken out when all of it, its operands too, is inside one, the ranges in any order and overlapping, each operation found in them by one search. Each is counted an operation at a time, so a reader stepping through content finds several in one pass: `Balance::take`; `MarkedNest` (`new`, `step`, which tells the range an operation opened or ended, `MarkedEvent`, by its index, `get`, `open`, the ranges still open, and `done`); and `NameSet::take`, from `Operation::resource`, the resource an operation names, by category, and its name. Every pass gives the operations' error, where it is.
