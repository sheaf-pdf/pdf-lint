# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (unreleased)

- **Tokens.** `tokens(bytes, &ResCx, &Limits)` gives a content stream's tokens in order, a `Tokens` iterator (fused, `Clone`, `Send` and `Sync`) of each token's range and its `Tok`: `Op`, an operator, any word that isn't a number, `true`, `false` or `null`; `Operand`, an operand's bytes as written, a number, a name, a string, an array or a dictionary with all it holds, or a closing delimiter with nothing to close; or `InlineImage`, `BI` to `EI`, one token, with its data's range. Between them is only white-space and comments, and after an error nothing comes. Tokens end where PDFium's `CPDF_StreamParser` ends them: at white-space and delimiters, a word of digits, signs and points a number, strings nested, and an array or a dictionary one token.
- **Inline images** ended by PDFium's `ReadInlineStream` rules, by the first that applies: unfiltered, by the data's exact size (`/W`, `/H`, `/BPC` and its color space's components, rows padded to a byte; an image mask's one bit of one component; a name the resources don't have read as none); `/AHx`, `/A85` and `/RL` to their end-of-data markers; `/DCT` to the JPEG's end of image; `/L` or `/Length`, where given; then on to the next `EI`. With none, the first `EI` with white-space before it and white-space, a delimiter or the end after. Only the first filter counts, and abbreviated keys and names read as the full ones. A JPEG's end of image is searched for once for the images that share it, or that find none. `ResCx` gives the components of the color spaces the resources name (`ResCx::new`, `ResCx::none`), and `family_components` counts a family as the lexer does.
- **Operations.** `operations(bytes, &ResCx, &Limits)` and `Concat::operations` give a content stream's operations in order, an `Operations` iterator (fused, `Clone`, `Send` and `Sync`) of `Operation`s, its tokens grouped: each operator, or inline image, with the operands since the last, and after an error nothing. An operation's operands' ranges are held in the item (tinyvec's `TinyVec`), sixteen inline, as many as PDFium keeps, so an operation of no more allocates nothing; more spill to the heap, every operand kept up to `Limits::operands`, past which is the operand cap's error, where the first past it starts. Operands at the end, which no operator takes, are no operation. `Operation`: `name`, the operator as written (`BI` for an inline image); `at`, where the operator is, or the image, `BI` to `EI`; `range`, from its first operand; `operands`, in order, an exact-size iterator; `arg(i)`, operand `i` from the last, as PDFium takes them, none past the first for any `i`; and `inline_image`. Its `Debug` shows the operation, not the content.
- **Inline images as operations.** `InlineImage`: `at`, `dict` (what's between `BI` and `ID`), `data`, `entry(keys)`, the value under any of the keys, as written, the last such entry as PDFium keeps it, a key matched with its `#` escapes decoded; and `length`, its `/L` or `/Length` where that's a length, as its data's end is found from it.
- **Operands as written.** `Operand`, an operand's bytes and where they are (`bytes`, `range`), `Copy`: `name` and `object` read it as the functions of those names do; `elements`, an array's elements as written, each an `Operand` where it is, found as they're asked for (`Elements`), none for what isn't an array; and what kind it is: `is_number` (a word of digits, signs and points, as PDFium tells a number), `is_name`, `is_string`, `is_array`, `is_dict`, from how it starts.
- **Operands.** `name` decodes a name operand's `#` escapes, borrowing it when there are none, a `#` without two hex digits kept as written; `object(operand, depth)` reads an operand as a pdf-objects `Object`, unplaced: an integer or a real by Rust's reading of the word, `true`, `false`, `null`, a name decoded, a string's bytes in its `Form`, and arrays and dictionaries read in one pass, a dictionary keeping every entry, as `Dict` does, and passing over an entry whose key isn't a name; none for what isn't an object, or one nested deeper than `depth`.
- **A page as one.** `Concat` joins streams with a space between each, as PDFium joins a page's, one stream borrowed as it is: `bytes`, `stream_count`, `stream`, `range`, `pos` and `offset` (a joined offset to a `Pos`, a stream and a place in it, and back), `span` and `offsets` (a range to a `SpanAcross` and back), and `tokens`. `Join` joins them as they're read, under `Limits::content`: `push`, `push_with`, which hands a decoder the joined bytes to append to and the room left (`room`), cutting back to where they were, the space too, when it fails or writes past its room, and `finish`. A stream pushed, an empty one too, is the next stream, so a `Pos`'s stream is the index of the stream given. Past the cap is `TooLarge`; `push_with`'s refusal is `Pushed`, the cap's or the decoder's error.
- **Caps and errors.** `Limits` (`#[non_exhaustive]`, set on a copy of `Limits::DEFAULT`): `operands` before an operator (65,536), `depth` of arrays and dictionaries (512, PDFium's), `inline_image`'s data (64 MiB), and a page's `content` joined (256 MiB). `Error`, an `ErrorKind` and the offset where what's wrong starts, displayed as the kind and its byte: a string, hex string, array, dictionary or inline image that doesn't end, nesting past the cap, operands past it, an inline image's data past it; and `Marked` and `Emc`, for marked content that doesn't end and an `EMC` that ends none. Derived with thiserror; `ErrorKind` is `#[non_exhaustive]`. Never a panic, whatever the bytes.
- **Tests.** Unit tests for tokens as written, names' escapes, operands read as objects, an operand nested 500 deep around 8 MiB read in one pass, inline images by each rule, errors and where they are, each cap at the cap and one past, content of 50,000 inline images that don't end read in one search, random content tiled by its tokens, streams read as one and their places mapped back, an error's place in its stream, a join's caps, its decoder's room and its refusals, and the types `Send` and `Sync`; operations as their tokens grouped, operands from the last and past the count, every operand kept to the cap (16 held inline, 17, 33 and 65,536 spilled) and the cap's error at the first past it, an inline image's operands before `BI`, its dictionary, entries and length, operands at the end no operation, an error ending them, a page's operations mapped back to its streams, and operands' kinds, names, objects and elements; the README's example and the items' as doctests; and a fuzz target (`just fuzz content`) whose body, which unit tests run on mutated seeds and random content, reads its input whole and as two streams, joined alike, its tokens tiling it, each operand read, its operations its tokens grouped, each operand where its token is and `arg` agreeing with `operands`, an array's elements tiling it and read as the array reads them, and again under small caps, the same up to the cap's error.
